📊 Buying call triggers (auto-monitored)

The tracker automatically monitors these signals and fires alerts:

● Near 52W low — Stock within configured % of 52-week low → accumulation zone
● Breakout watch — Stock within configured % of 52-week high → momentum entry
● Big drop — Intraday fall beyond threshold → potential panic selling = buying opportunity
● Volume spike — Unusually high volume (3x+ avg) → institutional activity
● Stop-loss breach — Stock breaks below key support → exit signal

Jyoti Resins specific triggers:
● CMP below ₹850 → Strong buy zone (promoter buying range)
● CMP above ₹1,000 → Breakout confirmation (watch for volume)
● CMP above ₹1,167 → 50 DMA reclaim = trend reversal confirmed
